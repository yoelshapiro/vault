Parking Capability — Architecture Research

The release driving model is a stateless, fixed-window policy: 1 s of video in, 2 s of trajectory out, no memory across ticks. The task: extend it to the full parking ladder — street parking through multi-story lots, gates, preferred spots, and memory parking (R1–R6). Basic parking is reachable with curated data; the harder tiers expose three structural gaps — a too-short horizon (G1), unhandled multimodality (G2: which spot, which maneuver, when to commit), and no memory (G3: searched area, lot rules, stored spots). This report is the full, adversarially-reviewed proposal: six solution families that compose into one architecture, kept production-viable under the frozen-trunk multi-head deployment paradigm.

TL;DR — seven pieces, one architecture

The network stays a stateless parking head on the frozen shared trunk. Everything that must persist lives in an external Parking Memory Service and re-enters as tokens. A slow ~1 Hz strategic loop chooses where to park and via which legs; the fast tick loop only executes the committed leg. Click a card to jump to the full design.

P0 · PREREQUISITES
Representation decisions first
The three recorded street-PUDO failures are representation symptoms, not bugs: add HOLD/dwell semantics, fix termination contracts, run a one-week reverse diagnostic, unify everything on one gear-aware LegCode vocabulary.
S1 · PRX HEAD
Propose → Rank → eXecute
One stateless head that makes the spot/maneuver choice explicit, rankable and conditionable — anchored-truncated diffusion restores on-car multimodality in ~2 denoise steps.
S2 · LEG CODEBOOK
Long horizon as structure, not length
A maneuver becomes an AR sequence of gear-delimited legs (POLICY_PARKING_LEGS); autoregression at the leg level only, single-shot within a leg, 2 s controller contract untouched.
S3 · MEMORY AS INPUTS
Parking Memory Service
Coverage, spot inventory, rules, stored spots and the committed plan live outside the network and re-enter the parking head as tokens — inspectable, replayable, retrain-proof.
S4 · DATA ENGINE
Fleet logs → parking supervision
HER/GCSL goal relabeling, GPS-resolution lot priors, first-vs-second-lap contrast pairs, and a VLM attribute pipeline — scoped to what survived the data-realism critique.
S5 · DECISION LAYER
Critic-as-ranker + commitment
The existing C51 critic re-deployed as a shadow-first plan ranker (V-GPS pattern), plus hysteresis so a stateless network stops changing its mind every tick — with an explicit abort/recovery state machine.
S6 · RIDERS & GYMS
Other teams' trains, made visible
WFM latent-action pretraining is off the 2026 critical path; 3DGS lot gyms are promoted (the only credible source of search-behavior signal); gates are behavior + ops, not architecture.
HONESTY CLAUSE
Mostly disciplined integration
By design: the brief is production, not publication. The defensible novel bits are (a) the gear-aware leg-code vocabulary under the frozen-trunk constraint and (b) memory-as-input wiring with an explicit commitment layer.
