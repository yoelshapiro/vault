# Parking Solutions — Adversarial Critique Log (2026-06-12)

Companion to [[parking-capability-architecture-research]]. A first draft of the §8 solutions was reviewed by four adversarial critics (production/deployment, training & data realism, novelty/coherence, organizational fit), each with read-only repo access. All blockers/majors below are **already folded into §8 of the main doc**; this log preserves the findings and evidence for traceability.

## Production / deployment lens — blockers
- **Route-raster compositing corrupts a control signal:** end-of-route detection sums route-raster pixels (channels 0–1 vs threshold, `deployment_wrapper.py:3452-3469`) and auto-triggers `PARKING_MODE`. Painting coverage/spots/stored routes into the raster shifts the sums → suppressed or spurious parking-mode flips. Fix: migrate end-of-route to nav-instruction distance inputs first.
- **Frozen trunk can't read new raster semantics:** `RouteInputAdaptor` is a frozen `Conv2d(3,…)` upstream of the trunk; parking-head gradients can't teach it. Fix: parking-head-side tokens/cross-attention, or pre-freeze trunk channel reservation.
- **PMS precedent claim wrong:** indicator memory is an in-graph TRT buffer of a few scalars (`persistent_state.py:156`), not an external service; PMS as specced is a new safety-relevant on-car subsystem + a relocalization stack that doesn't exist in the repo (mapping stack is tile rendering, not SLAM).
- **Mode switch vs trunk temporal cache:** interleaved engine swap needs both engines warm (peak load at lot entrance; cold cache on abort, in reverse, near pedestrians). Fix: single multi-head engine + `PARKING_MODE` flag selection.
- Majors: K-proposal re-ranking every tick churns plans (no commitment mechanism existed in the draft); data-dependent beam search doesn't compile to TRT (fix: 1 Hz strategic loop or fixed-width unrolled beam); critic may be asymmetric (second perception trunk on-car) and is forward-driving-calibrated; localization drift (5–30 m over 1–2 km) misregisters coverage by 1–2 aisles (fix: ~500 m window, drift injection, soft prior); GPS circularity across S3/S4 in garages; dual Python/C++ renderer parity priced as free; rule-cache staleness needs a conservative-veto runtime semantic.

## Training & data lens — blockers
- **Coverage "free labels" was the weakest claim in the doc:** (1) causal hindsight rasters are just the ego's past trail — a shortcut feature with no counterfactual pressure (drivers re-enter covered areas, so the empirical mapping is contradictory); (2) the draft's S3 (causal) and S4 ("hindsight") renderings were ambiguous — the whole-run variant leaks the expert's future loop; (3) search segments train with `parking_mode=False` (50 s/30 m window, `parking.py:295-396`) but deploy with it ON. Fix: plumbing-validation only; behavior from paired-contrast mining + 3DGS-gym RL with exploration reward; on/off ablation gate.
- **Cross-run spot aggregation assumed pose association that doesn't exist:** GNSS 5–20 m vs ~2.5 m spot pitch, absent in garages — contradicting the draft's own localization budget; counterfactual positives need cross-run pose <0.5 m AND knowledge the spot is free now (circular with the perception being trained). Fix: GPS-resolution priors only; recall-only candidate supervision with free-space gating.
- **gc buckets ≠ leg labels:** gen2 gear is reconstructed from speed (0.5 km/h threshold, dwell heuristics — `filters.py:130-200`) exactly at leg boundaries; windows clip at 25 s/30 m truncating gc3plus; buckets are frame indices, not leg structures; volumes unverified (config upweighting betrays gc3plus scarcity); three gear-reconstruction implementations disagree.
- Majors: **P→D forward-unparking detector gap poisons `unparking_window_gc1` (weight 0.3, the heaviest parking bucket)** — fix sketched in the code comment, unscheduled in the draft; goal-dropout story partly fictional on main (CFG is a TODO; the 50% dropout lives on a branch) and **unparking carries `goal_distance=NaN`** → no goal-conditioned unparking data (breaks "R6 needs no new pathway"); factorized vocabulary imbalance (rare cells empty; hindsight hard assignment → confident garbage) + `enable_latent_action=False` everywhere (the conditioning pathway the draft assumed is disabled today); VLM BEV-polygon grounding is the PSD failure mode restated (and redundant with the PSD campaign); PUDO contamination (no unparking nopudo; 2 s parked-duration threshold admits lane stops).

## Novelty / coherence lens
- **Novelty claims trimmed:** CVaR/distributional plan ranking is established academically ("unpublished in production" is unfalsifiable — claim deleted); counterfactual fleet positives = standard fleet auto-labeling; PRX = TNT/DenseTNT + Hydra-MDP + DiffusionDrive + MultiPark glued (fine for production — sold as integration); leg-AR *is* MultiPark; stored-route-as-conditioning is Valeo/Xpeng/RT-Trajectory prior art. Surviving novel bits: gear-aware unified leg-code vocabulary under the frozen-trunk constraint; memory-as-input wiring with commitment layer.
- **Composition conflicts:** three competing goal-injection mechanisms (anchor-init needs a retrain; affinity guidance has no schedule room at 2 steps and fights anchors → demoted to offline tool; trained goal token + in-graph inpainting kept); aWTA and anchored diffusion are the same multimodality device twice (aWTA → contingency gate); three latent-action vocabularies → unified to one leg-code; S6 contradicted the frozen-trunk paradigm and wasn't in the phasing.
- **The recorded failure modes were the least-served part of the draft:** standstill-in-drive ⇒ distance-parameterized paths can't express "wait" (HOLD/dwell semantics needed — gates' creep-and-wait was cited while proposing a representation that cannot encode waiting); end-of-route fires on every park (termination semantics undefined); reverse instability got tokens, not a diagnosis (trunk features may never have represented rearward motion — forecloses head-side fixes).
- **Missing entirely:** un-park cold start; near-field safety case (<30 cm, sub-FOV obstacles); mode-switch/abort state machine; controller co-design (2 s extraction at gear cusps, reverse tracking); eval metrics + volumes; ramp driving; lot etiquette.

## Organizational fit lens
- **Trunk-input gating:** every draft memory pathway entered through trunk inputs while coverage training was phased *at* the MS4 freeze — exactly backwards. Fix: per-input freeze-boundary decision; head-side conditioning by default.
- **S6.1 can't fit 2026** and S2 secretly leaned on it (resolved: leg codebook = BC-head output decomposition).
- **Phasing was off by ~1 milestone everywhere**; "Now–MS3" was triple-booked against MS2 delivery; S1's proposer was scheduled a milestone after the roadmap's PSD head (must consume it instead).
- **Resource collisions:** three annotation campaigns → one schema/queue; rear-camera dependency unstated; no DRAM/parameter budget vs the ~25%-per-head limit; critic ownership undefined (shared vs per-head copy).
- **Branch revival risk:** Wonjoon's code survives only under `origin/aa/wonjoon_*` (author off-project; unreproduced result vs a pre-diffusion-merge model); `AnnealedWTALoss` port is a workstream; Soham's PR is live — coordinate, don't annex.
- **MPA scope creep:** the stored-spot layer pre-decided the end-Q2 reassessment → demoted to a one-page input. USS safety shell (the one roadmap item with explicit safety scope) was absent → added as execution bound + leg-replan semantics.
- Minimal high-value "do now" set (adopted in §8.9): P→D detector fix, count audit, reverse diagnostic, critic offline prototype (replaces `confidence=1.0`), end-of-route migration, trunk-slot reservation decision, support #114772, timeboxed Wonjoon spike.
