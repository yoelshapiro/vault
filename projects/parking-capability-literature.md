# Parking Capability Research — Literature Digest

Companion to [[parking-capability-architecture-research]]. Six-topic sweep, 2026-06-12. Each entry: mechanism + how it transfers to our stack (stateless ViT+ST transformer, 1 s in / 2 s out, 961-way latent-action grid, distance-parameterized diffusion path head). Full per-topic reports archived in the session transcript; this is the curated digest.

## 1. Diffusion / flow planners & controllability

- **DiffusionDrive** (CVPR 2025 Highlight, [2411.15139](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15139)) — **truncated diffusion from anchored Gaussians around K mode-anchors**: 2 denoise steps, per-mode confidence, real-time. *The single most applicable recipe: our latent-action grid / spot candidates are the anchor vocabulary; fixes both step count and the zero-noise single-proposal collapse on car.* **DiffusionDriveV2** ([2512.07745](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.07745)) adds RL fine-tuning + RL-informed ranking — matches our BC→RL.
- **DRIP** ([2510.14000](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14000)) — initialize DDIM denoising from a pretrained RL policy's action distribution at truncation step ~5/25; +10% over diffusion-QL on the HOPE parking benchmark. *Near drop-in for our 5–10-step head.*
- **GoalFlow** (CVPR 2025, [2503.05689](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05689)) — goal-point vocabulary scored *before* generation, flow-matching generator conditioned on the chosen goal, trajectory scorer after; competitive at 1 step. *The spot-multimodality-with-ranking blueprint; goal vocab can live in external memory.*
- **Diffusion Planner** (ICLR 2025 Oral, [2501.15564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15564)) — energy guidance at inference adapts behavior without retraining; closed-loop nuPlan SOTA + real fleet transfer. *Best closed-loop evidence that post-hoc guidance survives control — supports Soham's affinity guidance direction.*
- **Diffusion-ES** (CVPR 2024, [2402.06559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06559)) — sample → score with black-box reward → mutate via partial renoise; beats gradient guidance on nuPlan. *The alternative when rule/memory costs are non-differentiable (occupancy, discrete spot validity).*
- **Decision Diffuser** (ICLR 2023, [2211.15657](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.15657)) — CFG conditioning channels **compose at test time**; conditions must be designed in with dropout. **Diffuser** (ICML 2022) — inpainting pins endpoints zero-retraining.
- Fast sampling order of preference: anchored truncation → flow-matching + **Shortcut Models** (ICLR 2025, step-size-conditioned, one training run, runtime quality/latency dial) → consistency distillation (mode-sharpening risk).
- **MultiPark** ([2508.11537](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11537), production-deployed on QC8650, 78.95% closed-loop) — parking paths decomposed at **gear-shift points**; AR next-*segment* prediction; factorized semi-anchors **gear(2) × longitudinal(3) × lateral(5) ≈ 32 modes** with classification scores; curvature-arclength chunks (RK2); imitation → outcome-loss finetune. *The best published answer to multi-leg maneuvers; most production-credible number in the sweep.*
- **ParkDiffusion/++** ([2505.00586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00586)) — signed velocity encodes reversing; kinematic feasibility layer after diffusion; ego-intention conditioning.

## 2. Multimodal prediction & discrete-mode selection

- **MTR lineage** (NeurIPS 2022 → MTR v3; WOMD champion 2022/2023/2024) — fixed intention-anchor vocabulary + **hard-assignment CE** + anchor-conditioned refinement + calibrated top-K. *Most production-proven "K discrete goals + confidence + decode" recipe.*
- **TNT/DenseTNT** ([2108.09640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.09640)) — goal candidates from priors → classify → goal-conditioned decode; DenseTNT's **offline hindsight goal-set pseudo-labels** are the battle-tested fix for min-over-K instability. *Parking has a perfect hindsight label: the spot actually parked in.*
- **WTA failure modes & fixes:** pure WTA → hypothesis collapse; ε-WTA → spurious mean-modes. Fixes: EWTA annealing, **aWTA** ([2409.11172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11172)) temperature-annealed soft assignment — *which `zmurez/pudo`'s `AnnealedWTALoss` independently reproduces* — EDA distinct anchors (AAAI 2024), mmTransformer region partitioning, ModeSeq sequential mode decoding (CVPR 2025).
- **Hydra-MDP** (NVIDIA, CVPR-challenge winner 2024, [2406.06978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06978)) — trajectory vocabulary scored by heads **distilling rule-based teachers** (collision/drivable/comfort) + imitation. *The recipe for rule-aware spot ranking.* **GTRS** (2025 winner, [2506.06664](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06664)) — scorer trained to generalize to off-vocabulary proposals (e.g. diffusion samples).
- Calibration of mode heads: systematic overconfidence; post-hoc temperature scaling per domain; *no dedicated driving-mode-calibration paper 2024-25 — open gap.*
- **ParkPredict+** (ITSC 2022, [2204.10777](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.10777)) — intent distribution over candidate spots + multimodal trajectories; DLP drone dataset. *Only direct spot-selection-as-intent precedent.*

## 3. E2E parking — academic & industry

- **ParkingE2E** (IROS 2024, [2408.02061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02061), 87.8% real-garage) — **target-slot-as-query** conditioning of a BEV policy. *The cleanest published interface between spot selection and an e2e policy.* ParkFormer, E2E-Parking CARLA benchmark ([2504.10812](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.10812v1)): gear tokens in the action space work; dataset iteration mattered as much as architecture.
- **HOPE** (T-ITS 2025, [2405.20579](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20579)) — RL hybridized with Reeds-Shepp via an **action mask**. *Action-masking = cleanest way to inject kinematic/rule feasibility as an external input.* **RL-OGM-Parking**: rule-based planner for coarse approach + RL for terminal precision (two-regime split).
- **ParkingWorld** ([2605.25029](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.25029v1)) — 3DGS reconstructions of real lots as RL gyms + correction-in-the-loop RL; 80% real.
- **Industry memory parking** (the R6 reference designs): **Xpeng VPA/VPA-L** — *one human demo run* stores route + visual-SLAM feature map on-vehicle, no GPS/no HD map, cross-floor since 2023, ~100 stored spots; **Valeo** published the seminal version ([2001.02161](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.02161)): store trajectory + sparse fisheye feature map, relocalize-and-track; **Aptiv** Memory Park Assist — stored map *refreshed on every replay*. **Huawei ADS** — "roam if occupied" productized search behavior; fleet-built lot priors. **Tesla** — vision-only spot proposal + user tap (UI = the cheapest multimodality ranking), FSD v13/14 navigates unmapped garages with no stored route (reviewer-reported).
- **Gates/barriers:** Li Auto OTA 6.5 ships barrier-gate handling as **perception + creep-and-wait** (plus ETC); Bosch/Mercedes L4 AVP (ISO 23374 Type 2) dissolves tickets via **operator backend/ANPR**, no robot-pulls-ticket anywhere. *Gates are a data/behavior + ops problem, not architecture.*

## 4. External memory & online mapping (stateless-compatible)

- **Pattern verdict:** three battle-tested external-memory representations: (a) ego-warped latent BEV tiles (**Neural Map Prior**, CVPR 2023) — highest fidelity but model-version-coupled & pose-sensitive; (b) **decoded vector/instance memory** (**MapTracker** ECCV 2024 Oral — raster+vector buffers with element IDs; **PrevPredMap** — memory = the model's own previous *predictions*) — compact, inspectable, survives retrains; (c) **token queues** (**StreamPETR** ICCV 2023 — ego-motion-compensated past query tokens). 2024→25 trend: away from latents toward decoded/token memory. *For a fleet with frequent retrains: decoded memory, rendered as rasters or tokens.*
- **ChauffeurNet** (Waymo 2018, seminal) — policy consumes rendered top-down raster stack; *the precedent for "memory enters as rendered layers, not state".*
- **Coverage:** frontier exploration (Yamauchi 1997); **MemFusionMap** (WACV 2025) observation-count heatmap channel; **SemExp** (NeurIPS 2020) multi-channel semantic episodic maps beat RNN memory; **VLFM** (ICRA 2024) value-map-ranked frontiers, deployed on Spot. *Explicit coverage maps reliably beat learned recurrent memory for search.*
- **Rules:** **PAMR** (AAAI 2026, [2509.22756](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22756)) — Map-Rule Cache keeps parsed sign rules in effect after the sign leaves view; **MapDR** (CVPR 2025, [2410.23780](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23780)) — sign→structured-rule benchmark, F1 ≈ 0.65 (arrows 0.44) ⇒ *offline extraction with QA, not online.*
- **Localization budget:** wheel+IMU dead reckoning ≈ 1.5% drift; VIO/VIW with loop closures <1% (Kimera-AVP, 8 km garages); **AVP-SLAM** (IROS 2020) ~2.4 cm against a stored semantic map; **VIPS-Odom** — slot detections as SLAM landmarks (the PSD head doubles as the localizer). Floor: differential barometer (sub-meter) + ramp counting + pillar OCR ([OCR-RTPS](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10489-022-04362-x)) — never integrated VIO z. ⇒ *coverage rasters at ≥2 m cells are drift-tolerant; stored spots must be keyed semantically and re-anchored.*

## 5. Hierarchy, latent actions, test-time search

- **Why hierarchy:** SuSIE (ICLR 2024), HIQL (NeurIPS 2023 — short-horizon advantage has better SNR), Horizon Reduction/SHARSA ([2506.04168](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.04168) — flat offline RL saturates; only horizon-reducing methods scale at 1B transitions). *Keep the 2 s executor; the proposer's subgoal arrives as an external input (DriveVLM-Dual async slow/fast interface).*
- **Latent-action pretraining:** **LAPA** (best paper CoRL-WS 2024) — VQ latent-action prediction from actionless video, >30× pretraining efficiency; **caveat** ([2502.00379](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.00379)): collapses under traffic distractors unless semi-supervised with ego odometry (we have it). **TAP** (ICLR 2023) — state-conditional VQ trajectory codes + **beam search over code sequences** at 20 Hz. *Multi-leg = beam over leg codes.*
- **Chunking:** ACT ablations (1%→44% success k=1→100, drop beyond); chunking theory ([2507.09061](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.09061)) — compounding error drops from exp(horizon) to chunk-rate; **Q-chunking** (NeurIPS 2025) — TD in chunk space. **OpenVLA-OFT** ([2502.19645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19645)) — parallel decode beats AR (+14%, 25–50× faster): *AR only at the leg/segment level, never per-step.*
- **Test-time search:** **V-GPS** (CoRL 2024, [2410.13816](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13816)) — frozen offline-RL value function reranks K samples from any policy, no retraining. *Our C51 critic is exactly this; CVaR-risk-aware ranking of parking plans is unpublished white space.* **Centaur** ([2503.11650](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.11650)) — cluster disagreement across samples as an uncertainty/fallback gate. **Hindsight relabeling** (HER/GCSL): every fleet park is goal-conditioned training data; failures become ranking negatives (GCSL-NF). **RT-Trajectory** (ICLR 2024) — hindsight trajectory *sketches* as conditioning prompts ⇒ stored-route-as-overlay for memory parking.

## 6. Parking-domain semantics

- **PSD:** published SOTA is fisheye/AVM-bound (PS2.0, DMPR-PS, **CRPS-D** 2025 — largest real dataset + semi-supervised recipe, public). **BEV-PolyNet** (2025): polygon slots from learned BEV features (no fisheye IPM) — *evidence a slot head on our ViT/ST latents is plausible.* With 6 pinhole cams, corner-accurate PSD at 10–25 m is unrealistic → **two-tier head**: coarse gap/occupancy candidates at range (Tesla pattern), precise 4-corner polygons <10 m. Label schema: 4-corner polygon + entry edge + type {regular, EV, disabled, reserved, …} + occupancy {free, occupied, partially-blocked, unknown}. Only 9/26 surveyed works predict occupancy — gap we'd own.
- **Signs→rules:** detector + VLM offline into MapDR-schema structured rules with human QA; online only a coarse "restriction present" flag. AWS/Azure parking-sign study (2025): symbols F1 0.99 but **arrows/scope 0.44** — scope grounding is where label budget goes.
- **Datasets:** CRPS-D (PSD), SUPS (sim garage, pinhole+fisheye+SLAM GT), **DLP** (drone, 5k agents — spot-choice behavior), ParkPredict+/ParkDiffusion baselines, E2E-Parking CARLA benchmark, MapDR (rules), AccessParkCV (aerial disability-spot priors).

## Cross-cutting white space (unclaimed in literature)

2. Mode-probability calibration for driving mode heads (2024-25 gap).
3. Counterfactual cross-run spot supervision from fleet logs (spots other runs used, visible but untaken in this run).
4. Pinhole-only adjacent-lane PSD benchmark — would need to be built.

## Marketing-claim flags
GoalFlow "1-step 90.3 PDMS" and DiffusionDrive "10×/3.5×" are open-loop NAVSIM numbers; TrajHF 83.2% is vendor-evaluated; Xpeng "zero interventions", Huawei "500k lots", Baidu "85% activation" are marketing; MultiPark's 78.95% on-vehicle and Bosch/Mercedes's approved deployment are the most credible production numbers; Tesla unmapped-garage claims are reviewer-reported, method unpublished.
